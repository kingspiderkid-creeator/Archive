A private viewer for your downloaded Instagram data.
Works with the JSON or HTML export.

Drop your export folder or .zip here
Nothing is uploaded. Everything is read and displayed inside this browser tab.
Get your data in Instagram: Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information (pick JSON or HTML).
Large export in several zips? Unzip them into one folder, or add the zips one after another.
